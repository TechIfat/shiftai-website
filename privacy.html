The ComplyAI self-assessment

Your answers stay in your browser. If you choose “Book a call”, your score and the gaps flagged are added to the Calendly link, so Calendly and we can see them. Nothing is sent unless you click.

The research crawler and Evidence Index

The index is about organisations, not people. To build it, our crawler collects publicly available documents: NHS trust web pages, published board papers, and public procurement and transparency records.

These original documents may contain names and job titles of staff, because they appear in published papers. We hold the originals privately, only so that quotations can be checked against their source. We never publish personal details. Email addresses and phone numbers are removed from the text we keep for analysis, and any record containing personal details is blocked before review.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): building an accurate, verifiable record of how organisations govern AI.

Originals are deleted after 12 months unless they back a published record. If you are named in a document we hold, you can object or ask us to delete it by emailing hello@shiftaiconsulting.co.uk. See also our research crawler page.

Who processes your data for us

  • IONOS: email.
  • Render: runs the back end of the contact form, which passes your message on to us by email. We do not keep a copy of your message on it, but it logs technical data such as IP addresses.

  • GitHub Pages: website hosting. It may log technical data such as IP addresses.

GitHub Pages, Calendly, Render and Resend may process data in the United States. They rely on the safeguards in their published data protection terms.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies, and fonts are served from this website, so visiting it does not send your details to Google.
