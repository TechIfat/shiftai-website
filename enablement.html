What the day covers

09:30–17:30. Around six hours 45 minutes of session time.

Built from your own pre-session diagnostic answers (who's using Claude Code, for what, what's configured, what's currently ungoverned), not a generic deck.

09:30 · 45 min

Where you actually are

The team maps its own current usage on the wall: who uses it, for what tasks, what's configured, where data is going. Everything after this is specific to what's on that wall.

10:15 · 45 min

The delegation principle

Use the model for semantic reasoning over unstructured text, and delegate arithmetic, verification and decision-gating to deterministic code you can test.

11:00 · 15 min

Coffee break

11:15 · 75 min

Build your CLAUDE.md

Hands-on, not a template handout. The team writes theirs: coding standards, approved libraries, security rules, project context.

12:30 · lunch

Lunch

13:15 · 60 min

Permissions, secrets and data classes

What Claude may touch, what must never leave the building, and review-before-apply workflows. The person accountable for security needs to be in the room.

14:15 · 45 min

Cost and model routing

Why token spend escalates quietly, what session discipline looks like, and routing strategy. The team leaves with a written usage policy.

15:00 · 15 min

Afternoon break

15:15 · 60 min

Patterns that compound

Skills, subagents, and structuring work so month two is better than month one rather than the same.

16:15 · 45 min

Capstone: one real task, end to end

The team picks a genuine item from its own backlog and runs it through everything from the day.

17:00 · 30 min

The 30-day plan

Pilot group named, task categories agreed, what gets measured, and when you review.

What you walk away with
