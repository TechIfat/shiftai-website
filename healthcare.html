The shared challenge

Regulated AI asks the same three questions.

Financial services and healthcare differ in their rules, not in what a reviewer wants to see.

01 / Accountability

Financial servicesNamed senior individuals are accountable for AI-assisted decisions.

HealthcareDCB0129 (for manufacturers) and DCB0160 (for deploying organisations) set clinical risk management expectations for health IT.

02 / Evidence trails

Financial servicesA regulator can ask how one decision was made.

HealthcareClinical safety documentation, data protection impact assessments (DPIAs) and, for suppliers, DTAC all depend on clear records of what a system does and why.

03 / Human oversight

Both sectorsA person should be able to review, challenge and override an AI-assisted output, and you should be able to show that they did.

For public-sector transparency, the Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard (ATRS) is the UK government’s standard for publishing information about algorithmic tools. It is mandatory for central government departments and certain arm’s-length bodies, and recommended more widely across the public sector.

Sources: DCB0129 and DCB0160 (NHS England); DTAC (NHS AI and Digital Regulations Service); DPIAs (Information Commissioner’s Office); ATRS (GOV.UK). This is a comparison of approaches, not legal advice. Standards change, so check the current version of each.

How we work

Controls built into systems,
not only written in policy.

A policy says what should happen. A control makes it happen, and leaves a record. It is the approach behind ComplyAI, our financial services system.

01 / Verified evidence

Every claim is traced to a source you can inspect, and every quotation is checked against that source.

02 / Human review

A person reviews each record before it is relied on.

03 / Fail-closed checks

If a check cannot confirm something, the answer is no, not “probably”.

04 / Audit trails

A record of who decided what, when and why.

Common questions

Straight answers.

Are you NHS-approved or affiliated?
No. ShiftAi Systems Ltd is an independent company. We are not approved by, endorsed by or affiliated with the NHS, NHS England or any NHS organisation, and we do not certify or approve anything.
Do you need access to patient data?
No. We work from your policies, system documentation and process descriptions, and we will not ask for patient data. If an example would help, we will use one that contains no patient information.
Can you act as our Clinical Safety Officer?
No. DCB0129 and DCB0160 require a Clinical Safety Officer to be a suitably qualified and experienced clinician with current registration with an appropriate professional body, nominated by your top management. We support your Clinical Safety Officer by helping you prepare evidence and governance records. We do not sign off clinical safety cases.
Do you work with suppliers?
Yes. We support suppliers with AI governance for their product and their team, including preparation for DTAC. The submission, and every claim in it, stays with you.
How does an engagement start?
With a free 45-minute call. If a readiness review looks worthwhile, we agree a fixed scope in writing before any work begins. If it does not, we will say so.
What will the index publish, and when?
Nothing yet. We have not published any findings and have no publication date. Nothing will be published until each record has been checked against its source and reviewed by a person. Corrections and opt-out are covered on the research crawler page.
Do you work remotely?
Yes. We work remotely across the UK by video call, and can meet in person by arrangement.
